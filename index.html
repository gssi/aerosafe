      Framework workflow

      Six project stages, not six checklist items

      The stages explain how a project moves through the 52 controls. They do not replace the item-level checklists.

      Audience 1

      Assurance planner / configurator

      Chooses groups, tailors controls, fixes evidence and criteria, assigns owners and gates, and defines traceability and closure before results are judged.

      Open the construction view

      Audience 2

      Reviewer / checklist user

      Records the evidence actually reviewed, result, finding or waiver, formal disposition, traceability links, and residual limitations.

      Open the execution view

      Guided project mode

      Turn “How to apply Aerosafe” into a transparent recommendation and project record

      Answer seven project-context questions. The deterministic recommender explains which checklist groups are baseline or context-triggered. Review or override every suggestion, then configure and execute the selected controls.

        Evaluation question VQ1

        Can the generic IMVV vertical be instantiated without hiding evidence or authority gaps?

        The bounded spacecraft-FDIR application keeps published detector evidence separate from an author-defined autonomous-reconfiguration extension. It tests structural instantiability and transparency, not qualification success.
